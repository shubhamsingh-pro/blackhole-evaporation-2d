Not a full general-relativity calculation: gravity is the Paczyński–Wiita pseudo-Newtonian potential, the emission rule is the standard Hawking scaling (T ∝ 1/M, dM/dt ∝ −1/M²), and the real lifetime of even a small black hole is astronomically longer than τ. See the README.
